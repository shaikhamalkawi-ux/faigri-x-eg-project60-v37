## Admission decisions

- Government of Canada AI Register: admitted for bounded structured-disclosure, lifecycle, and schema-gap analysis.
- Canada AIA: admitted as a qualitative instrument and construct comparator; not assumed applicable to all register records.
- EDUCAUSE QuickPoll: admitted as qualified aggregate procurement context; no respondent-level, institution-linked, or representative prevalence inference.
- IMDA pilot: context only; no detailed public outcome matrix.
- Pending outreach: recorded but not used in the current calculation.

## Excluded claims

No source is used to validate current case labels, calibrate weights or thresholds, certify compliance, infer implementation, rank institutions, estimate prevalence, or establish causal lifecycle effects.
