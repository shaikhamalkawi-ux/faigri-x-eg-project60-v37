## Government of Canada AI Register

The retained normalized transport copy contains 412 released register records across 43 organizations. Lifecycle status is non-empty for 370 records: 182 In development, 160 In production, and 28 Retired; 42 records do not report status. The deterministic education/training subset contains 33 records: 21 In production, 9 In development, and 3 status not reported.

Selected released-field counts are 412/412 descriptions, 410/412 primary users, 382/412 developer types, 308/412 capabilities, 294/412 data sources, 275/412 notification fields, and 296/412 results fields. Among 88 records marked as involving personal information, 31 have a non-empty personal-information-bank reference. In the 33-record education/training subset, 11 records are marked as involving personal information and none has a non-empty personal-information-bank reference in the released fields.

These results describe the release schema. They do not establish compliance, quality, implementation, safety, readiness, or absence of controls.

## Schema and instrument comparison

The register has no dedicated released field for fairness/accessibility or human review/appeal. Privacy/data governance, transparency/limitations, and accountability/risk receive partial structured support. The Canada Algorithmic Impact Assessment contains richer risk and mitigation constructs, so register inventory and impact assessment are treated as distinct layers.

## Higher-education procurement evidence

The EDUCAUSE aggregate reports 49% current AI-aware procurement and 10% planned adoption. Among institutions with a process, 36% report ongoing review. Security/privacy and legal compliance are each considered by 86%, algorithmic bias by 30%, teaching-and-learning participation by 58%, technology participation by 94%, and cybersecurity/privacy participation by 85%. Twenty-one percent identify opaque contract terms as a challenge.

These are rounded, anonymous, non-institution-linked publication aggregates and are not external validation or prevalence estimates.
