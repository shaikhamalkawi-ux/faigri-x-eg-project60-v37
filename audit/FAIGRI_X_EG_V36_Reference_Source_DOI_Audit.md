## Source treatment

The Canada dataset URL and resource URL are retained in the acquisition manifest and transport rows. EDUCAUSE aggregates retain poll dates, response count, instrument-question count, license, denominator qualifiers, and claim boundaries. IMDA is cited only for pilot-scale counts.

## Citation boundary

Official sources support the reported public-data facts. A normalized Canadian transport copy is provided for reproducibility but does not supersede the official open-data record. Outreach emails are not scientific sources and are not cited as evidence.
