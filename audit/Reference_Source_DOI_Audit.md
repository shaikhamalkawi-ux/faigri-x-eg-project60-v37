## Dynamic-source boundary

Official vendor and institutional webpages can change. The package records source identifiers, canonical URLs, access dates, item-level evidence notes, and locations, but does not claim byte-level historical reconstruction when snapshots are unavailable.

## Method-reference boundary

Fuzzy-set and TOPSIS references support the ancestry of the coding convention and comparison method. The manuscript does not claim propagation of fuzzy uncertainty after symmetric centroid conversion.
