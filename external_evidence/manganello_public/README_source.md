# Manganello XAI-ED CAF public supplementary materials

This package was created from the official Frontiers supplementary PDF for:

Manganello, F., Nico, A., Ragusa, M., and Boccuzzi, G. (2025).
Testing the applicability of a governance checklist for high-risk AI-based
learning outcome assessment in Italian universities under the EU AI Act Annex III.
Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, 8, 1718613.
DOI: 10.3389/frai.2025.1718613.

## Contents

1. `Manganello_XAI_ED_CAF_Official_Supplementary_Material.pdf`
   - Official 67-page Frontiers supplementary file.

2. `01_screening_dataset_100_universities.csv`
   - Public-web screening dataset covering 100 legally recognized Italian universities.

3. `02_xai_ed_caf_checklist_27_items.csv`
   - The 27 checklist questions across seven ALTAI dimensions.

4. `03_consensus_item_scores_and_coding_notes_378_rows.csv`
   - 14 universities x 27 checklist items.
   - Includes the published consensus score and qualitative coding note for each item.

5. `04_scoring_rubric.csv`
   - Published 0-3 item-scoring definitions.

6. `Manganello_XAI_ED_CAF_Public_Materials.xlsx`
   - Consolidated workbook for analysis.

## Critical methodological boundary

The published supplementary material provides consensus item-level scores and
qualitative coding notes. It does not provide the four separate pre-consensus
coder matrices, anonymized coder identifiers, or a disagreement-resolution log
at coder-row level.

Accordingly, these files support reproduction and audit of the published
consensus dataset, but they do not independently permit recomputation of
intercoder reliability from raw coder ratings.

## Extraction note

The CSV files are machine-readable transcriptions of the official PDF. The
