# Public reproducibility, admission and source limitations

This release reconstructs the deterministic FAIGRI-X-EG calculations **starting
from frozen adjudicated case-level inputs** (`data/case_input_matrix.csv`,
`data/mandatory_safeguard_matrix.csv`). It does not reconstruct historical
source-to-score coder sheets or independent human agreement, and it must not
be described as complete end-to-end raw-source reproduction.

The author-created public source-to-claim trace records 16 propositions from
nine selected documents. Exact verification of captured third-party source bytes
requires original private snapshots; only hashes or public pointers are presented
here. No unauthorized complete institutional/vendor pages are redistributed.

For the Government of Canada register, the original source of record is
https://open.canada.ca/data/dataset/fcbc0200-79ba-4fa4-94a6-00e32facea6b
and the admitted transport normalization is retained with its own hash and
licence field, **not** asserted to be byte-identical to the official export.

The Manganello official supplementary PDF included in the source materials has
CC BY 4.0 licensing as described in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICE.md`; include attribution
and do not misstate its consensus data as independent validation.

No raw identifiable student-level data, real procurement decisions, secured
institutional files, personal mail or author declarations are included.

Reproduction commands for core, UK and Canada from the frozen admitted input
are described in README. Some scripts have outputs and plot rendering that
may vary in bytes across environments; physical findings/counts are frozen.
