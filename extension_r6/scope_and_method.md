This is a purposive, single-analyst documentary extension of V36.1. It is not a blinded preregistration, an independent-coder study, or validation against historical institutional decisions. The analyst had already inspected the R5 sources.

The unit is an explicitly scoped proposition and its cited document, not a university or product rating. Six comparison families concern procurement authority, participation and operational activity, conditional permission, feature/contract scope, document authority/time, and outcome inference. Sixteen analytical traces use nine source objects from the seventeen archived in the twenty-source R5 attempt. They are illustrative contrasts selected for their evidentiary roles, not a population denominator or a classifier test set.

The existing M0–M7 meanings remain unchanged. The treatment column qualifies a proposition and is NOT a new M code, risk indicator, weight, score or dossier-level Veto outcome. No new case-specific mandatory inventory is invented. Computational checks verify bytes, source anchors and arithmetic; they do not establish semantic agreement between independent reviewers.

The snapshot is 15 October 2026. Three failed raw captures remain on Hold. Later web pages must not replace these bytes silently. The full original snapshots remain in the private Project 60 Drive archive; redistribution rights are not assumed.
